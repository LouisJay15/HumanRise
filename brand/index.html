02Our story

Hiring is about people, not paperwork.

HumanRise started with a simple belief: hiring is about people, not paperwork.

Behind every CV is a person with ambitions. Behind every vacancy is a team trying to grow. Too often the process between them is rushed and impersonal. Good people are filtered out before anyone has spoken to them, and businesses pay for it with hires that don’t last.

We do it differently. We listen first. We match on skills and on fit. We take care of the HR and compliance detail so owners and managers can get back to running their business. We use modern tools, AI included, to work faster and more carefully, and a person always makes the call.

Our name says what we do. Human, because people come first. Rise, because our work is about helping people and teams go further than they thought they could. Our promise sits underneath it: Overcoming Limits.

Purpose · why we exist

To help people and teams overcome their limits.

Mission · what we do

We connect great businesses with great people, and give both the HR support, training and guidance they need to grow.

Promise · our tagline

Overcoming Limits.

The idea in the logo

Read our logo slowly and it tells the whole story. Every detail was drawn on purpose.

  1. 1

    “Hum” starts soft

    Lowercase, approachable, in Human Lavender. This is the person as they are today, standing on the line.

  2. 2

    The star takes the place of the “a”

    Hand-drawn, because we are people: imperfect, and we fit in. A star, because everyone has the potential to be among the greatest. Your potential is full and great.

  3. 3

    After the star, the “n” rises

    It lifts off the baseline (dashed line) and turns vivid purple. Meet your potential and you rise. It also echoes the rising I below.

  4. 4

    RISE in capitals

    Larger and stronger than “Human”. RISE is the outcome we reach together, and a call to act: rise up.

  5. 5

    The I breaks through

    The darkest letter lifts off the baseline and breaks through the top line of the word. It is the individual rising and a ceiling broken. It is Overcoming Limits, drawn.

  6. A+I

    Two changed letters spell AI

    The only letters we redrew are the A (the star) and the I. Together they say we are forward-thinking and use AI usefully and safely, in our own way. The human star comes first.

The colour flows like a career

Purple is new, fun and creative. Blue is trust, experience and stability. Read the logo top to bottom and the colour moves from soft lavender, to vivid purple, into blue and finally the deepest blue of the rising I. It grows and flows the way a career does.

Human-led. AI-assisted.

The A and the I in our logo are a promise about technology. We use AI where it makes us faster, fairer and more careful: sorting applications, drafting documents, scheduling, spotting patterns. People make the decisions that affect people. This is how we keep our website promise that “a real consultant, not a bot” reviews every CV.

A person makes the call

A HumanRise consultant reviews every CV, shortlist and HR recommendation. AI supports that work and never replaces it.

We’re open about it

When AI plays a part in a process that affects someone, we say so in plain language.

Data stays protected

We only use tools that meet our POPIA obligations, and we keep personal information out of tools that don’t.

gavel

This also keeps us in line with section 71 of POPIA, which limits decisions about a person that are based solely on automated processing.

People first

We treat candidates and clients as people, not transactions, with honesty at every step.

Shows up as: skills and culture-fit matching, psychometric testing, and checking in well beyond day one.

03Logo

One mark, used with care.

Our logo is a stacked wordmark: “Hum★n” above “RISE”. It is one piece of artwork. Always use the master files and never rebuild, retype or rearrange it.

Logo versions

Full colour (primary)

The default. Use it on white and on the lightest tints (Mist, Sky, Lavender 100).

White (reversed)

On Deep Rise, Star Purple, Purple 600–700 and dark, calm areas of photos.

Single colour, Deep Rise

One-colour print, embroidery, engraving, stamps and branded pens.

Black

Only when print is limited to black: newspapers, faxed or photocopied forms.

Star mark

The star on its own, for favicons, avatars, watermarks and small spaces. It never replaces the logo on a first introduction.

Clear space

X is the width of the rising I in RISE. Keep a clear space of at least 2X on every side of the logo, which is about 10% of the logo’s width. Nothing enters that space: no text, edges, folds or other logos.

On screens, never use less than 24px, whatever the logo size. In print, never less than 6mm.

Setting rules

  • Sentence case for headings and buttons: “Book a consultation”, not “Book A Consultation” or “BOOK A CONSULTATION”.
  • Left-align text. Centre only short headlines on covers, banners and social posts.
  • Highlight one idea per headline in Star Purple, as in “Overcoming Limits.”
  • Emphasise with Medium or Bold, not italics or underlines. Underline links only.
  • Keep lines between 45 and 75 characters, and use tabular figures in tables.
  • Never stretch, outline or add shadows to type.

Fallbacks and everyday tools

  • Web: load Roboto from Google Fonts. Stack: 'Roboto', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
  • Google Docs and Slides: Roboto is built in.
  • Microsoft Word and PowerPoint: install Roboto from fonts.google.com. Use Arial for files you send out for others to edit.
  • Email signatures and HTML emails: Arial, because most inboxes don’t have Roboto.
  • Print: send PDFs with fonts embedded.
06Graphic elements

The star, the path and soft edges.

A small kit of shapes that we can use anywhere and that still feels like us. The hand-drawn star is the hero of the kit.

The star

The star from our logo works on its own as a brand device. It is drawn as one continuous line, with two small loops that keep it human and a little imperfect. It is always an outline, never filled. Every colourway is drawn at 82% opacity, which gives the line its soft, pencil-like feel. Keep that opacity, and don’t make it solid.

Star PurpleIcons, value cards, accents
Deep RiseFormal documents, print
Rise BlueSupporting accents
FaintLarge watermarks on white
WhiteWatermarks on purple and blue

Use the star

  • as a large watermark, cropped by the edge of the page, post or card (one per layout)
  • as a small icon beside values and key points
  • as the avatar and favicon
  • as the “a” stand-in, but only inside the logo itself

Don’t

  • redraw, trace, smooth or “fix” the line
  • fill it in, add a shadow or put it in a solid shape
  • scatter lots of stars as a pattern or use it as a bullet in long lists
  • spin it, or use it to rate things (“5 stars”)

The rising path

A dotted trail joins numbered markers to show progress through steps. Use it only for real sequences: how a process works, a course outline, a timeline. The trail is 2.4px Lavender 200 dots, and markers are white circles with a Rise Blue ring and a Deep Rise number.

  2. 2

    We shortlist

    You get a curated shortlist of screened, fit-matched candidates.

07Photography

Real people, real work, real light.

Our photos show the moments we make possible: a good conversation, a team that clicks, someone stepping into a new role. They are bright, natural and warm, and they reflect the people of South Africa.

Look for

  • Diverse South African faces, ages, abilities and body types
  • Real workplaces across our sectors: offices, shop floors, factories, warehouses, training rooms
  • Natural light, light backgrounds and soft, true colour
  • Candid moments of listening, learning and working together
  • Calm space in the frame for a headline or logo

Avoid

  • Staged stock clichés: thumbs-up, pointing at screens, jigsaw pieces
  • Cold corporate scenes: empty glass towers, suits only
  • Heavy filters, dark moody grading or strong colour casts
  • Too many handshakes. One per page at most.
  • Visible CVs, ID numbers, payslips or any personal information

Text on images

Put text on a calm area of the photo, or add a Deep Rise overlay that fades from 85% to 0% across the image. Text on photos is white, with headlines in Roboto Black. Round photo corners to 18px on screens.

policy

Consent comes first. Only photograph or publish real candidates, clients, learners or staff with their written permission, in line with POPIA. Record the consent with the image. Credit stock photos where the licence asks for it.

08Voice & tone

We sound like a capable friend.

Professional, clean and modern, and always human, warm and supportive. We write short, clear sentences that lead to an action. No corporate jargon.

Four voice principles

1. Talk to people

Write to one person, using “you” and “we”. Say people, team and candidate, not “human capital” or “headcount”.

2. Keep it clear

Short sentences, everyday words and one idea per paragraph. If a 15-year-old wouldn’t understand it, rewrite it.

3. Be warm and honest

Show empathy, especially when the news isn’t good. Never overpromise. Only publish facts and figures we can prove.

4. Lead to action

Tell people what happens next and what to do: “Submit your CV”, “Book a seat”, “Request talent”.

Tone by situation

Our voice stays the same. Our tone adjusts to the person and the moment.

11Checklist

Before anything goes out.

Ten quick checks for every post, advert, document or partner piece.

  • The logo comes from the master files, unaltered, with 2X clear space and above minimum size.
  • Full colour on white or light tints, white on dark colours. No mid-tones, no cream.
  • Colours come from the palette, with purple as the spark and plenty of white.
  • Text passes contrast: Ink, Deep Rise or Star Purple on white, and white on Deep Rise or Star Purple.
  • Type is Roboto (Arial for email and editable files), with headings and buttons in sentence case.
  • No more than one star, used as an outline, unfilled and not redrawn.
  • Photos show real, diverse people in natural light, with consent recorded.
  • Copy is short, warm and jargon-free, and ends with one clear action.
  • Facts and figures are true and provable. AI is described as assisting people, never deciding.
  • Legal lines are in place: the POPIA statement, the registered name where needed, and QCTO only on accredited courses.
